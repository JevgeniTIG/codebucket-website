Who keeps this list?

Codebucket is a small studio in Tallinn, Estonia. We take an idea from a sentence on a list to something people use every day — web platforms, SaaS products and mobile apps, designed, built and shipped by the same people.

Codebucket in brief

What does Codebucket do?
Codebucket designs, builds and ships software: web platforms, SaaS products and mobile apps for iOS and Android. The same small team takes a project from the first idea to a live product.
Where is Codebucket based?
In Tallinn, Estonia. The company is CodeBucket OÜ, registry code 17281724.
What has Codebucket built?
Web front-ends for Tradefora, an execution-analytics platform for brokers and traders; MERE 44, a 3D sales site for a sea-view plot near Tallinn; Votumio, its own SaaS for live polls, quizzes and retrospectives; and Decanted plus five smaller apps on the App Store and Google Play.
Does Codebucket build mobile apps?
Yes. Its apps ship to both the App Store and Google Play from one codebase, released with the same version on both stores.
How do I start a project with Codebucket?
Describe your idea in the form at the end of the list on codebucket.ee, or write to info@codebucket.ee. You get a reply by e-mail.
